### Fixed
- A Discovery preset scan no longer shows nodes from the preset you are not scanning. The scan's own filter was being switched off immediately after it was switched on, so a scan of MediumSlow spent its five minutes drawing the ordinary node list - VIA MQTT group included - instead of what the radio heard while it was parked.
- A scan result stays scoped to the scan now that its window has an end as well as a start. The result is deliberately left on screen after the radio comes home, and everything heard on your own preset afterwards used to walk straight into a list headed with the scanned one.
- HEARD ABOUT no longer fills a scan with a bridged mesh's topology. NeighborInfo reports now record whether they arrived over MQTT, and Discovery leaves those out - on the screen, in a saved snapshot and in both counters.
- While a preset scan has the radio parked on another preset, nothing crosses between the two meshes: MQTT downlink, MQTT uplink, relaying, the periodic NodeInfo, position and telemetry announces and the MQTT map report are all held until the radio is home. Announces are only deferred, not skipped.
- A packet that arrived just before any retune is no longer handed up as though it came in on the new settings, which could open a scan with a packet from the preset it just left.
