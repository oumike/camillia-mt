### New
- Nodes: a **Search** button at the top of the list (Space on keyboard builds) opens a Search dialog with a text field, Cancel and Search. It matches part of a long name, a short name or a node id (`!1a2b`), ignoring case, and lists every matching node. Archived nodes are included only while Show Archived Nodes is on.
- Nodes: while search results are shown, the button reads **Clear search**. Tapping it, or pressing Space or Backspace, takes the list back to favorites.
- Nodes: on keyboard builds, pressing Up from the first row (or from an empty list) moves the cursor onto the Search / Clear search button. Down moves it back, and Enter presses the button.
- Direct Messages: on every board except the T-Lora Pager and the T-Display P4 in landscape, the conversation list is now a drawer that slides in from the left over the messages, like the chat's channel list. Open it with C (H on the Cardputer), the DM button again on the ThinkNode M9, or on touch screens by tapping the name in the header or swiping in from the left edge.

### Changed
- Nodes: the list now shows only your favorite nodes until you search, and the title reads `Favorites (n)`. To add a node to your favorites, search for it and favorite it from its actions menu. Every visit to the screen starts on the favorites.
- Nodes: typing straight onto the list no longer filters it. Searching now goes through the Search dialog on every board, where Enter searches and the close key cancels. The on-screen keyboard appears only on touch-only devices that have no physical keyboard attached.
- Direct Messages: on the drawer boards, the screen opens full width on your most recent conversation and names that contact in the header. Picking a conversation, tapping outside the list or pressing C/H again closes the drawer. With no conversations yet, the screen opens on the list at New DM.
- Direct Messages: opening a DM from the Nodes screen, or starting a new DM, goes straight into the conversation on the drawer boards.
- T-Deck Pro: the DM drawer opens and closes instantly instead of sliding, to avoid extra e-paper refreshes.
