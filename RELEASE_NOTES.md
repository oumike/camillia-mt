### Changed
- Maps: the zoom and center buttons are twice as large and easier to tap, with bigger labels and zoom level text.
- CrowPanel Advance 3.5: in portrait, the bottom navigation bar is twice as tall and its icons are larger. Landscape is unchanged.

### Fixed
- LilyGo T-Display P4: the device no longer browns out or resets in the first seconds after boot when running from the keyboard accessory with Wi-Fi on and the screen bright. Wi-Fi now starts about 10 seconds after boot and transmits at up to 15 dBm, which slightly shortens Wi-Fi range.
